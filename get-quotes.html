Buying-request FAQ

Is posting a buying request really free?

Yes. Buyers never pay Trade.Place. We may earn referral or listing fees from partners, which keeps the service free for you.

Who will see my request?

Only suppliers or service providers relevant to your product and route. We do not publish your name, email or phone number publicly.

How many quotes will I receive?

It depends on the product, quantity and destination. Specific, realistic requests with clear specs and quantities attract the most responses.

Does Trade.Place guarantee suppliers?

No. We screen listings, but we are not a party to your contract. Always verify the company, inspect goods and use secure payment terms.
